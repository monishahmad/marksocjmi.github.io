Why we exist

Four gaps on campus

No dedicated marketing hub

Students lack a specific platform for marketing interests, so this booming career domain stays underexplored on campus.

Skill gap

Students lack practical skill development and resume-building opportunities, which affects employability.

No real-time experience

Minimal interaction with real brands means theory dominates and portfolios stay thin.

Untapped potential

Students are capable but lack the exposure and mentorship to compete nationally.

Our work

Projects we have delivered

A selection of client work from the Society, and many more beyond it.

My Daily Goods

Designed and executed an end-to-end Instagram marketing strategy. Directed every phase from ideation and scripting to visual production, delivering three distinct campaigns that build a brand narrative.

Forelsket Softwares Pvt Ltd

Led a complete social media transformation, managing everything from creative direction to final production to drive engagement and inspire action.

Urban Bond Tea

Developed a 90-day growth and e-commerce strategy. Deliverables included brand repositioning around 100% Pure Assam CTC, packaging redesign, Meta Ads architecture and a targeted B2B scale-up plan.

Bharat Innovation Challenge

Led brand marketing and promotional strategy, creating content and strategic deals to maximise student participation and build wide awareness of the initiative.

Medixcy

Delivered marketing consultancy and a full brand revamp with a modernised colour scheme, plus an end-to-end Instagram Reel shoot to drive engagement and awareness.
